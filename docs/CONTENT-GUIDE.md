# Writing and design guide

Prepared for this redesign on 16 October 2026.

The goal is readable, credible content in Iron Key's voice. These principles are not a claim that typography, punctuation or any detector can prove who wrote a page.

## What the research supports

GOV.UK's tone guidance recommends specific, clear, conversational language and the active voice. We apply that by saying what Iron Key does and speaking directly to founders and prospective managers, rather than filling the page with abstract promotional language. [Source 1]

Nielsen Norman Group's web-writing research supports concise, scannable and objective content. We apply that with descriptive section headings, short paragraphs, a clear navigation directory and company descriptions visitors can understand quickly. [Source 2]

Stanford's credibility guidelines emphasize verifiable information, visible people and expertise, easy contact, useful design and restrained promotion. We apply that through the actual portfolio, Joseph's sourced background and the named venture partners. No invented metrics, testimonials, endorsements or founder quotes have been added. [Source 3]

Nielsen Norman Group distinguishes informative imagery from decorative filler. The redesign retains the supplied city footage and explanatory animations. Joseph and the partner directory are text-led rather than illustrated with unrelated stock portraits. [Source 4]

W3C guidance describes the need to let people pause certain automatically moving content. The city video and Fish animation now have pause controls and honor a reduced-motion preference. This is a specific improvement, not a certification that every part of the site conforms to WCAG. [Source 5]

## Iron Key's house style

Write as the firm would speak in a useful first conversation. Use active verbs and natural contractions. Keep the main meaning at the beginning of a paragraph. Vary sentence length when the meaning benefits, rather than mechanically making every sentence short.

Use one main heading for each section. Do not add a small label above it that simply repeats the same point. A genuine list of method steps can still be numbered. Form labels, role descriptions and useful metadata are not decorative heading labels.

Keep headings descriptive. Not every section needs a slogan. "Our portfolio" and "Our venture partners" are enough. Do not repeatedly use contrived contrasts, identical three-part sentences or promises to "unlock," "revolutionize" or "seamlessly transform" something.

Do not use em dashes. Use a full stop, comma, colon or parentheses where appropriate. A source check enforces this preference. This is a house-style choice, not evidence about AI authorship.

Be specific about the work. Explain a company's product and the role of a program. Avoid claims about investment returns, fundraising outcomes, assets under management or customer traction unless approved and sourced.

Describe the studio, investing and portfolio as part of Iron Key today. Do not frame them as an abandoned earlier business. The 2022 date provides context without turning the homepage into a pivot story.

Define "Neofund" before describing its layers. Make clear that an operating model does not replace the legal structure of an investment vehicle. Separate what Fish is building toward from capabilities confirmed to be available now.

Do not invent full names for partners whose public profiles use initials. Their employer names explain professional background, not an employer endorsement. Get approval before adding portraits, quotes or more detailed biographies.

## Design applications

Use different layouts for different material: an editorial studio introduction, a portfolio directory, a founder profile, a partner directory and an interactive explanation. Avoid imposing the same cards and heading pattern on every section.

Keep the existing palette, motion artwork and typographic character. Use motion to show something or establish atmosphere, not to obstruct reading. Keep action labels literal, such as "Explore the program" or "Start a conversation."

The headline, portfolio, Joseph and partners are visible evidence of Iron Key's identity. Fish has one dedicated homepage section, plus its factual entry in the six-company portfolio. It does not have a navigation tab.
